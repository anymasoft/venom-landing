FAQ

Questions, answered

Which countries does this work in?

Venom VPN is designed for high-censorship environments where standard VPN protocols get detected and blocked. It works globally, including in countries with active DPI-based filtering.

Is it legal to use a VPN?

VPN software itself is legal in the vast majority of countries. Local laws vary — you're responsible for complying with the regulations of your own jurisdiction.

What devices are supported?

Official apps for Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, using standard, unmodified VLESS clients.

How is this different from other VPNs?

Most VPNs use protocols that DPI systems can fingerprint and block. Venom uses Reality, which mimics a real website's TLS handshake — there's no VPN-shaped signature to detect.
